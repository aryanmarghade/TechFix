Refund & Replacement Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Hardware 7-Day Replacement

If a component arrives dead-on-arrival (DOA) or exhibits physical transit defects, customers can initiate a replacement within 7 days of delivery via their customer dashboard or support ticket.

2. Repair Diagnostic Guarantee

Repair diagnostics are fully transparent. If you decline an estimate after physical inspection, the device is safely returned with zero unauthorized charges.

3. Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled at any time prior to status reaching "Out for Delivery" by contacting our operations team.
