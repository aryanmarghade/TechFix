Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on orders, custom builds, diagnostics, and cash delivery.

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work?

You place the order without prepayment. Once the sealed package arrives at your verified address in Mumbai/Thane, you inspect the outer tamper seal and hand over the cash amount to the delivery executive.

How does the Custom PC Builder check compatibility?

Our configurator checks CPU sockets (e.g. LGA1700 vs AM5), RAM generations (DDR4 vs DDR5), estimated system wattage vs PSU capacity, and motherboard form factors in real-time.

What happens after I book a repair request?

A diagnostic ticket is created. Our engineering workbench inspects the hardware and issues a formal written estimate. Work begins only after you approve the estimate in your dashboard.
