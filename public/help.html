Orders & Cash On Delivery

How does Cash On Delivery (COD) work? ▼
When placing an order on TechFix, select Cash On Delivery. You do not need to make any upfront online payment. Our courier will deliver your items directly to your address in Mumbai/Thane. You can inspect the package exterior and pay the exact invoice amount in cash to the courier representative.
How do I track my order status? ▼
You can track your order in real time by visiting our Track Order page and entering your order number (e.g. TF-ORD-2026-1001). If you have an account, your full history is visible under My Orders.

Custom PC Builder & Compatibility

How does the TechFix PC Builder guarantee component compatibility? ▼
Our Custom PC engine cross-validates CPU sockets against motherboard sockets (e.g. LGA1700 vs AM5), RAM memory generation (DDR4 vs DDR5), motherboard form factors against cabinet chassis dimensions, GPU lengths against case clearances, and calculates cumulative power draw against your chosen PSU wattage.
Does TechFix assemble and test custom PC builds before delivery? ▼
Yes! Every custom PC ordered through our builder is assembled with cable management, thermal compound application, latest BIOS update, XMP memory profile enabling, and 24-hour stress benchmarks before dispatch.

Laptop & PC Repairs

How do repair estimates and customer approval work? ▼
After our technician collects your laptop or desktop and performs lab diagnosis, an exact repair estimate is updated on your portal. We never perform chargeable repairs without your 1-click confirmation or verbal approval.
