Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Service Coverage

TechFix currently provides dedicated doorstep delivery and pickup services across Mumbai and Thane pin codes (400001–400104, 400601–400615).

2. Delivery Charges

• Standard delivery: ₹99 flat rate.
• Free Delivery: Applicable automatically on all orders with subtotal ₹999 or higher.

3. Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is available up to ₹1,50,000 per order. Customers are requested to inspect the outer package seal prior to cash handover.

4. Packaging Standards

Sensitive electronic components (CPUs, GPUs, Motherboards, Pre-built PCs) are dispatched with ESD-safe antistatic packaging and multi-layered bubble wrapping.
